- `scripts/factory.py` — orchestrator, runs per-director or all

- `libs/tcrender/` — Mac-side wrapper around `ppttc.exe` headless (SSH to Windows-VM, render, ferry back)
- ppttc.exe runs on `Windows-VM` (SSH alias)
- DocumentFormat.OpenXml SDK installed on VM as alternative validation oracle

- Default `min_match_ratio=0.5`
- After stripping dev-instructions, found 84/194 strings → 56.7% missing → render fails
- The fix is either: lower threshold to ~0.3, OR scope verifier to chart-shape evidence only (not all text), OR rebuild verifier evidence list to exclude dev-instruction strings
- Backup of pre-strip seed at `assets/LAND_thinkcell_seed.pre-stripdev.pptx`
