## Implications for the KPI work

Every KPI in the RW/AP framework that touches `Apttus_Config2__AssetLineItem__c` (renewal coverage, ARR roll-ups, churn calc) is computed on a corpus where 31% of rows are stale-by-date. Either we filter to non-past-due in every query, or we surface this pollution explicitly.

Every KPI that touches `Apttus__APTS_Agreement__c` is computing on zero data — those KPIs need to be redirected to standard `Contract` (with `Status='Active'` filter to dodge the 13,904 abandoned `Created` records).
